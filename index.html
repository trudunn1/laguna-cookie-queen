The Story

From Fashion to Flavor

After years in the fashion world, Karen brought her eye for detail and taste for the finer things into the kitchen. Her cookies are rich, salty-sweet, and a little glamorous — baked with french golden butter and crowned with just enough attitude.

Every cookie is homemade and baked to order in Laguna Beach. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just real ingredients, California sunshine, and a whole lot of love.

A taste of California confidence in every bite.
