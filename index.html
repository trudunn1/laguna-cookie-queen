The Story

From High Heels to Hot Ovens

After years in the fashion world, Karen traded high heels for hot ovens — and somehow became the Laguna Cookie Queen. Her cookies are rich, salty-sweet, and a little glamorous — baked with french golden butter and crowned with just enough attitude.

A taste of California confidence in every bite.

Every cookie is homemade and baked to order in Laguna Beach. No shortcuts, no preservatives — just real ingredients and a whole lot of love.
